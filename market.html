FAQMarketplace questions

    

    
Why are there no listings?
The marketplace contract is deployed and live. There are simply no listings yet — no companion has been listed. Listings appear here automatically once the first one is listed.

    
How do I buy without connecting a wallet here?
You don't buy on this page. Copy the buy prompt, paste it into your Binance Agentic Wallet / AI agent with the skill (SKILL.md), and the agent calls buy(tokenId) with the exact listing price.

    
Who gets the money when something sells?
95% goes to the seller and 5% royalty goes to the creator wallet. The split is enforced by the marketplace contract at the moment of sale.

    
Can I cancel or reprice my listing?
Yes — only the seller can cancel a listing or change its price, any time before it sells. Ask your agent to call the matching function on the marketplace contract.
