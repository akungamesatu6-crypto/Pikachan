## Game — "Piklet Run" (built 2026-11-19)
Canvas runner on the page: Piklet runs automatically, jump (Space/click/tap) to dodge storm clouds, collect Volt Orbs (+25 points). 3 lives, speed rises with score. Score is cumulative in localStorage.
- Every **1,000 total points = 1 milestone → 1,000 $PIKA** (claim via agent skill, cap 100,000 PIKA/wallet from the game = 100 milestones).
- Every **10 milestones (10,000 points) = 1 bonus free mint** (max 2/wallet, extra on top of the 1 Agent ID free mint, still subject to the FREE CAP of 1,000).
- Honest rule, stated on the page and in the skill: game scores are **self-reported (client-side)**; the contracts only enforce the per-wallet caps and do **not** verify scores on-chain (no backend signer).
