FAQQuestions, answered plainly

    

    
When does mint open?
Not yet — the contracts are not deployed. This page is a preview. The mint-open time will be announced in UTC when deployment is confirmed, and this page will switch from PREVIEW to live.

    
How much does it cost?
Mint #1 is FREE if you hold a SPACE ID Agent ID. Paid mints are 0.001 BNB flat. Max 6 per wallet (1 free + 5 paid). A 5-pack costs 0.005 BNB. The free mint is capped at 1,000 total across all wallets — after that, every mint is paid.

    
How do I mint without connecting a wallet here?
You don't mint on this page at all. Copy the agent prompt from the mint panel, paste it into your Binance Agentic Wallet / AI agent together with the skill (SKILL.md), and the agent calls mintFree() or mintPaid(qty) on the contract for you.

    
What can I pull from a free mint?
Free mints draw only from token IDs 1–1000 (Common through Epic). Paid mints draw from IDs 1–1110 (up to Legendary). ID 1111, the Golden Voltmouse, only appears in 5-pack draws.

    
How do game milestones work?
Play Piklet Run on this page. Every 1,000 total points = 1 milestone = 1,000 $PIKA (claim via the agent skill, cap 100,000 $PIKA per wallet). Every 10 milestones = 1 bonus free mint, max 2 per wallet, still inside the 1,000 free cap. Scores are stored in your browser and self-reported — the contract enforces only the caps, it does not verify scores on-chain.

    
What is $PIKA?
The collection's reward token: 1,000,000,000 total (540M pool / 460M treasury). 120,000 $PIKA per paid mint, max 600,000 per wallet. It is a reward token, not an investment — no return is promised.

    
What are the marketplace fees?
Seller keeps 95%, 5% royalty goes to the creator wallet. Listing is done through your agent with list(tokenId, price); buying with buy(tokenId) at the exact listing price.

    
Are the characters original?
Yes. All 17 characters — Piklet, Sproutle, Emberlet, Tidelet, Voltail, Petalhorn, Emberclaw, Tideback, Bloombeast, Infernodrake, Tidecannon, Frostphoenix, Thunderphoenix, Cinderphoenix, Psyguardian, Starpixie and Golden Voltmouse — are original artwork created for PikaCHAN.
